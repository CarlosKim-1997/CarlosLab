# Project Principles

## ACTIVE — Public evidence, private provenance

CarlosLab may show conclusions, public-facing classifications, counts, and sanitized idea/insight summaries. Raw personal conversation data and private provenance remain outside the public repository.

## ACTIVE — Explain before exploring

The Thinking Map must help a first-time visitor understand the recurring direction of thought before exposing the full archive. The archive is evidence for the explanation, not the landing experience itself.

## ACTIVE — Stable structure over monthly reinvention

Monthly analysis should extend an existing public ontology where possible. New categories, lineages, or recurring patterns require deliberate review rather than free-form monthly relabeling.

## ACTIVE — Human judgment remains the publication boundary

Automation may analyze, classify, generate snapshots, and prepare diffs. Publication remains a human-reviewed act.
